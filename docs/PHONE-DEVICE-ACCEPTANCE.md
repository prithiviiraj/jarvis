## Physical test prerequisites

- Owner identifies phone platform/model and the same private network as laptop. No mobile-carrier number is needed for this internet voice track.
- Existing certificate/key paths on laptop, matching selected interface IP, valid through session. Phone trusts the exact certificate normally, without bypassing a warning. No secrets pasted into a conversation.
- Local speech assets and loaded model ready on owner laptop, headset/output choice understood. Initial test uses non-sensitive wording.
- Owner reviews exact listener origin/interface/port/fingerprint and selects start. No firewall/tunnel changes implied.

## Evidence to collect only during the approved test

1. Phone page loads from exact trusted HTTPS origin, secure context without certificate warning. Pair request label/source reviewed on laptop.
2. Explicit phone microphone prompt succeeds. Record one0.1to30second turn, stop microphone on finish, verify local transcript and bounded audio reply. Check that no laptop audio plays automatically.
3. Follow-up question uses only this session's recent context. Verify selected local model and no cloud fallback.
4. Stop during recording, inference and playback. Late replies must not appear; old tokens/pair claims cannot work. Check fifteen-minute expiry and re-pair clears context.
5. Laptop busy conversation refuses a phone turn. Exact reviewed phone-to-desktop handoff preserves an existing draft and line breaks, never sends or speaks, and stops the listener.
6. Record errors and resource use. A single successful turn does not establish sustained latency, echo handling, interruption or outside-network access.

## Separate carrier-call blocker

Unlimited mobile recharge does not establish destination cost, international/premium/roaming coverage, Android companion behavior or permission to call someone. No working companion or carrier audio injection exists. Reviewed dial preparation is not call execution or delivery of words. Practical brother/business/restaurant calling requires owner-selected platform/transport, current costs and exact contact/content review, then native readback and audio acceptance. Do not label internet voice or Telegram audio as a carrier call.
